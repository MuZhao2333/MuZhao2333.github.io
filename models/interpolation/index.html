These are the original trained checkpoints. The decoder predicts atom types and coordinates; chemical bonds are inferred afterward. RDKit sanitization is a software validity check, not a guarantee of chemical plausibility. RMSD is reported only when RDKit can find a graph correspondence between the input and decoded molecule.

MolAlign3D uses the original split-UniMol epoch-14 checkpoint: 8 nodes, 16 structural channels per node. The 512-dimensional UniMol representation is split into eight 64-channel blocks; each decoder node receives 80 channels. Linearly mix latent geometry and complete decoder features between two endpoints, with optional proper-rotation alignment. The decoder seed is fixed across frames. Generated results may change chemical topology or fail sanitization.
