These are the original trained checkpoints. The decoder predicts atom types and coordinates; chemical bonds are inferred afterward. RDKit sanitization is a software validity check, not a guarantee of chemical plausibility. RMSD is reported only when RDKit can find a graph correspondence between the input and decoded molecule.

MolAlign3D uses the original split-UniMol epoch-14 checkpoint: 8 nodes, 16 structural channels per node. The 512-dimensional UniMol representation is split into eight 64-channel blocks; each decoder node receives 80 channels. Navigate the first two principal components of raw structural Zh; all other components remain at their source values. Directions are measured in standard deviations of the reference-set principal components. The same decoder seed is used across frames. Generated results may change topology or fail sanitization.
